# ajairu

Open-source tools, checklists, and resources for UK SMEs adopting AI and building bespoke software.

## What is ajairu?

ajairu is a founder-led technology consultancy helping UK small and medium enterprises get real value from AI and custom software. Two focused businesses, one shared mission: practical technology that moves your business forward.

### [ajairu.ai](https://ajairu.ai)

AI consultancy for UK SMEs. We help you cut through the hype and find where AI genuinely improves your operations. From readiness assessment to implementation, we focus on measurable outcomes, not science experiments.

- **AI readiness assessment** at [ajairu.ai/assessment](https://ajairu.ai/assessment)
- Practical AI strategy and roadmapping
- Hands-on implementation support
- AI governance and compliance guidance

### [ajairu.dev](https://ajairu.dev)

Bespoke software development for UK SMEs. When off-the-shelf tools do not fit, we build tailored software that fits your exact processes, integrated with the systems you already use.

- Custom web applications and APIs
- System integration and automation
- Technical due diligence and code audits
- Legacy system modernisation
